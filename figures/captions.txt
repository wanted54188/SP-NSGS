Paired $2\times2$ factorial on IC-1 and IC-2. Small hollow symbols retain every seed 2026--2028; larger markers and whiskers denote mean $\pm$ sample SD. Fig05_classical_neural_tradeoff.pdf
A-priori physical $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol q$ relative $L^2$ error versus genuine free-running mean vorticity relative $L^2$ error on IC-1 and IC-2. Hollow symbols are three neural training seeds or three classical sparse calibration splits; solid symbols and bars are their mean $\pm$ sample SD. Clark is deterministic and has no artificial SD. Neither axis is an observation-assisted result.
