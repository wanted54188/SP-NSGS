\section*{S7. Exploratory fixed-weight Clark--A0B1 blend}
The v3 IC-1 grid was $\alpha=0,0.05,0.1,0.25,0.5,1$, evaluated in both
reference-state a-priori and genuine free-running modes for three seeds.
The original sparse-only rule minimized the three-seed mean closure score
at the unchanged sparse validation locations and selected $\alpha=1$;
dense and rollout scores did not enter that rule. After inspecting IC-1
a-priori and free-running results, $\alpha=0.5$ was adopted as a new v4
hypothesis and locked before the six new IC-2 runs. It was not a
preregistered IC-1 optimum or the v3 sparse-selection outcome; the
pure-Clark rollout remains in the comparison. Historical IC-2 endpoint results
had already been inspected, so this fixed-parameter phase-realization check
is not a completely blind test. Both closures are evaluated on the current
resolved state at each RK4 stage and combined at the flux level; the
original projector, integrator and normalization are unchanged. No later
reference state enters the integrator. The reported 50-frame means include
the initial $t=0.1$~s state. The scaled A0B1 dissipative branch retains its
nonnegative transfer, while the composite correction does not inherit the
original limiter-radius bound. Table~\ref{tab:hybrid} and
Fig.~\ref{fig:hybrid} report the finite-case trade-off, including the small
and seed-dependent change in source error on evolved states.

On IC-1, the mean free-running vorticity error changes from
$0.589\pm0.009$ to $0.563\pm0.029$: the mean improves, but the
between-seed spread increases. Seed 2026 improves by only 0.0023.
These three-seed observations do not establish a universally robust or
statistically significant benefit.
